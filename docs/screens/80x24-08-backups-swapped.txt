 core/before-deploy: Back up before each deploy
 Sets [flk.backup] before_deploy = true and keeps more release images.
 During a deploy, after a machine stops and before it boots the new release,
 flk backs up its volumes. If the new release fails its checks, flk releases
 rollback --with-volumes can put the volumes back as they were on the old
 release. It needs a health check to judge a release failed.
